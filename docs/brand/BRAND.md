# Plate3D brand guide

Visual board: open `docs/brand/index.html` in a browser.

## Positioning

**Who we sell to:** independent and small-group restaurant owners in the US who care about presentation and want higher check averages.

**Promise:** diners see the real dish, at real size, on their own table, before they order. Fewer "what is this?" questions, more confident orders, more desserts and premium plates sold.

**Personality:** appetizing, confident, simple. We sound like a great host, not a tech company. The food is the hero; the technology stays invisible.

## Taglines

- Primary: **See it on your table before you order.**
- Short: **Your menu, true to size.**
- Sales line: **The menu that sells with its eyes.**

## Voice

- Say "see it on your table", not "augmented reality experience". Use "AR" only after the plain version.
- Short sentences. Concrete numbers (plate size, seconds to load, price) over adjectives.
- Talk about the diner's appetite and the owner's revenue, never about our stack.
- US English everywhere in the product.

## Color

| Name | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Espresso | `#1C1714` | Text, logo, dark sections |
| Porcelain | `#FAF6EF` | Main background (the "plate") |
| Linen | `#EDE5D8` | Cards, borders, subtle surfaces |
| Stone | `#6B625A` | Secondary text (5.5:1 on Porcelain) |
| Paprika | `#E8643A` | Brand accent, illustrations, accents on dark backgrounds |
| Paprika Deep | `#B93A15` | Buttons with white text (5.7:1), links on light backgrounds |
| Saffron | `#F3B63F` | Highlights, the "3D" badge (Espresso text on it, 9.8:1) |
| Basil | `#2E6A4E` | Success, "available", fresh/positive states |

Rules:
- Paprika is never used for small text on light backgrounds (3.3:1). Use Paprika Deep instead.
- Roughly 70% Porcelain/Linen, 20% Espresso, 10% Paprika + Saffron. The accent is a garnish, not the meal.
- Diner menus use each restaurant's own brand color. Plate3D colors appear on the landing page, the owner dashboard, the admin and sales material.

## Typography

- **Fraunces** (Google Fonts), SemiBold 600: headlines and the wordmark. Warm, menu-like serif.
- **Manrope** (Google Fonts), 400/500/700: UI, body text, numbers.
- Headline tracking slightly tight (-0.01em); body 16px minimum on mobile.

## Logo

- Mark: a plate on a table, in perspective, framed by AR scan corners. It reads as "a dish, seen through the camera".
- Files: `plate3d-mark.svg` (app icon, favicon), `plate3d-logo.svg` (light backgrounds), `plate3d-logo-dark.svg` (Espresso backgrounds).
- Minimum size: mark 24px, full logo 120px wide. Clear space: the height of the plate ellipse on every side.
- The wordmark is live text set in Fraunces. Outline it (convert to paths) before print or merch.
- Don't recolor the plate, rotate the mark, or put it on photos without an Espresso or Porcelain backing.

## UI cues

- Rounded corners 12–16px, soft shadows, generous white space: it should feel like good tableware.
- The "View on your table" button is always the most prominent element on a dish page.
- The "3D" badge: Saffron background, Espresso text, bold.
- Photography: warm, top-down or 3/4 angle, real wood or stone tables, natural light.
