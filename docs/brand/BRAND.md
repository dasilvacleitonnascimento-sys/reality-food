# Reality Food brand guide

Visual board: open `docs/brand/index.html` in a browser.

## Positioning

**Who we sell to:** independent and small-group restaurant owners in the US (starting in Miami) who care about presentation and want higher check averages.

**Promise:** diners see the real dish, at its real size, on their own table, before they order. Fewer "what is this?" questions, more confident orders, more desserts and premium plates sold.

**The idea in the name:** *Reality* — augmented reality, and the reality of the plate (true size, the restaurant's own dish, never a stock photo). *Food* — appetite comes first.

**Personality:** appetizing, precise, simple. We sound like a great host who also happens to be an engineer: warm about food, exact about size.

## Taglines

- Primary: **See it on your table before you order.**
- Short: **Real food. Real size.**
- Sales line: **The menu that sells with its eyes.**

## Voice

- Say "see it on your table", not "augmented reality experience". Use "AR" only after the plain version.
- Be exact: "a 28 cm plate", "12 oz cup", "loads in 2 seconds". Precision is the brand.
- Talk about the diner's appetite and the owner's revenue, never about our stack.
- US English in the product; Spanish menus for diners.

## Color

Two complementary heroes: **Tomato** (the food) and **Lens** (the camera, the measurement).

| Name | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Ink | `#15181C` | Text, logo, dark sections |
| Cream | `#FAF7F1` | Main background |
| Linen | `#EFE8DC` | Cards, borders, subtle surfaces |
| Stone | `#66605A` | Secondary text (5.8:1 on Cream) |
| Tomato | `#E5483A` | Brand accent, the plate in the logo, accents on dark (4.5:1 on Ink) |
| Tomato Deep | `#B8301F` | Primary buttons with white text (6.0:1), "Food" in the wordmark |
| Lens | `#0B7A70` | Measurements, rulers, AR corners, secondary buttons (white text 5.2:1) |
| Lens Light | `#7FD6C9` | Lens on dark backgrounds (10.5:1 on Ink) |
| Saffron | `#F4B63E` | The "3D" badge and highlights (Ink text 9.8:1) |

Rules:
- Tomato for appetite and action; Lens for anything about size, accuracy or the camera. Don't swap them.
- Tomato (not Deep) is never used for small text on light backgrounds (3.9:1). Use Tomato Deep.
- Roughly 70% Cream/Linen, 20% Ink, 10% Tomato + Lens + Saffron.
- Diner menus use each restaurant's own brand color. Reality Food colors appear on the landing page, dashboards, admin and sales material.

## Typography

- **Fraunces** (Google Fonts), SemiBold 600: headlines and the wordmark. Warm, menu-like serif.
- **Manrope** (Google Fonts), 400/500/700: UI, body text, numbers and measurements.
- Headline tracking slightly tight (-0.01em); body 16px minimum on mobile.

## Logo

- Mark: a plate in perspective framed by AR scan corners, with a dimension line underneath — "a real dish, seen through the camera, at its real size".
- Files: `reality-food-mark.svg` (app icon; also `app/icon.svg`), `reality-food-logo.svg` (light backgrounds), `reality-food-logo-dark.svg` (Ink backgrounds; uses Lens Light).
- Minimum size: mark 24px, full logo 140px wide. Clear space: the height of the plate ellipse on every side.
- The wordmark is live text in Fraunces. Outline it (convert to paths) before print or merch.
- Don't recolor the plate, rotate the mark, or place it on photos without an Ink or Cream backing.

## UI cues

- Rounded corners 12–16px, soft shadows, generous white space: it should feel like good tableware.
- The "View on your table" button is always the most prominent element on a dish page.
- Measurements always appear with a small ruler cue in Lens color.
- The "3D" badge: Saffron background, Ink text, bold.
- Photography: warm, 3/4 angle, real tables, natural light — and always the restaurant's own dish.
